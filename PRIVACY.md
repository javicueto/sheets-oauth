# Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

**Short version: this app collects nothing, stores nothing remotely, and sends
data to no one.**

## What it is

`sheets-oauth` is a personal Google OAuth client, used by its owner to let
locally-run tooling reach his own Google Sheets. It has no other users, no
server and no backend.

## What is accessed

Only Google Sheets spreadsheets belonging to — or already shared with — the
Google account that grants access, and only while the owner is running the tool
on his own computer.

## What is stored

An OAuth refresh token, on the owner’s laptop, in a file readable only by his
user account. Nothing else. No spreadsheet content is copied, retained, logged
or transmitted anywhere.

## What is shared

Nothing. There is no analytics, no third-party processor, no advertising, and no
sale or transfer of data of any kind.

## Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions — this immediately invalidates the
stored token.
